Engineering methodology

RSSI = transmit power + antenna gain − FSPL at 1 m − 10n log₁₀(d) − wall loss. A 1 m sampling grid evaluates the strongest AP. Shared walls are counted once per crossing; nominal wall losses are documented model assumptions. Capacity and interference are not yet simulated.
