Your information

Privacy.

Understand where your school plans are stored and how you control access to them.

01   Storage and accounts

Guest projects are stored in your browser. Signing in enables account-based cloud saving through Firebase; new signed-in projects can sync automatically. You can choose to keep a school local. Original imported floor images are stored locally rather than included in normal cloud sync.

School layouts, equipment details and saved measurements may be included in saved project data. Use fictional or approved information, and avoid entering student names or credentials.

02   Sharing and permissions

Private links are restricted to invited verified accounts. A shared school is a separate copy with its assigned view or edit permissions; a shared proposal contains the selected plan information. Choose whether to include maps. Revoking access does not remove copies or PDFs already downloaded.

03   External services

Firebase handles sign-in and cloud data, and Vercel hosts the website. Optional connection testing sends network requests to the test provider, which can receive your IP address and request data. Floor plan detection and PDF processing use browser libraries loaded from external servers; the app does not intentionally upload drawings to those providers.

04   Your choices

Export schools and proposals before clearing browser storage or deleting an account. Account settings include deletion of your cloud schools, owned shares and login. A minimal deletion marker may be retained to prevent subsequent writes. Local copies on other devices and copies held by recipients are not remotely removed.
